## 🎮 About the Game

Big Ambitions is a single-player business-management and life-simulation game developed by Hovgaard Games. It entered Early Access on March 10, 2023, and reached full release on August 28, 2026. Set in an open-world version of New York City, the game combines entrepreneurship, logistics, inventory management, time management, and personal needs.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Big Ambitions**. This trainer provides quality-of-life options for Big Ambitions, allowing players to adjust money, personal needs, time, and inventory-related values while building and managing businesses. It is designed for single-player use and complements the game's systems for renting properties, buying stock, operating stores, hiring employees, managing warehouses, and traveling across New York City.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with business layouts, test logistics networks, skip repetitive personal-needs management, explore the city freely, or focus on large-scale entrepreneurship without restarting their save.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Money** — Maintain a large cash balance for purchases, rent, payroll, inventory, vehicles, renovations, and business expansion.
- **Max Energy** — Keep the character's energy at maximum to reduce the need for sleep during demanding workdays.
- **Max Hunger** — Keep hunger filled while traveling, working, making deliveries, and managing businesses.
- **Max Happiness** — Maintain high happiness while reducing the impact of the character's personal needs on daily business activities.
- **Freeze Needs** — Prevent energy, hunger, and happiness from naturally decreasing during active play.
- **Freeze Time** — Pause the in-game clock while planning routes, arranging inventory, managing staff, or preparing a business schedule.
- **Adjust Game Speed** — Increase or reduce the passage of in-game time to make routine management, travel, and long workdays more convenient.
- **Inventory Support** — Assist with stock-focused gameplay by providing convenient control over item quantities used for personal supplies and business inventory.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Combat Mode** — Big Ambitions has no combat system. This trainer does not add combat features and focuses exclusively on business, life-simulation, and management mechanics.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Big Ambitions uses an isometric, top-down view of New York City. Move through streets and buildings on foot, or use vehicles and public transportation to reach stores, suppliers, warehouses, offices, and other locations. Freeze Time can help when planning routes or organizing a busy schedule, while Adjust Game Speed is useful during long trips and repetitive tasks.

### Tools & Inventory

Inventory is central to both personal survival and business operations. The character can carry goods and supplies, while stores and warehouses hold products needed for sales and distribution. Use Inventory Support when purchasing or testing stock, but remember that businesses still depend on appropriate locations, equipment, employees, suppliers, and logistics arrangements.

### World & Environment

The game world is a large, explorable version of New York City where the player can rent or buy properties, establish different types of businesses, purchase vehicles, hire employees, borrow money, buy products from suppliers, and develop warehouses and distribution networks. Trainer options are most useful when experimenting with expansion plans or testing a new business without financial limitations.

### Combat & Weapons

Big Ambitions does not include combat, weapons, enemies, or battle encounters. Progress is based on entrepreneurship, business operations, transportation, inventory management, employee scheduling, personal needs, and financial planning.
